Contactor


Contactor in English refers to an electrical switch that is used to control a high-power circuit with a low-power signal.
It works by using an electromagnet to open or close the contacts within the device.

Contactor components typically include


1. Coil: The electromagnet that generates the magnetic field when current flows through it.
2. Contacts:These are the components that open or close, allowing or interrupting the flow of electricity in the circuit.
There are typically three types of contacts: normally open (NO), normally closed (NC), and auxiliary contacts.
3. Armature: The movable part that is pulled or pushed by the magnetic field of the coil, activating the contacts.

Contactor applications include:



controlling motors, lights, heating systems, and other electrical devices,

typically in industrial settings.

They can handle high voltages and currents, making them ideal for switching operations where safety and reliability are critical.
