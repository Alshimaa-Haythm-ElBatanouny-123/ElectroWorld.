Timer



An electrical timer is a device that uses electrical components to control and manage the timing of electrical circuits.
It is designed to automatically switch a circuit on or off after a set period of time.
Electrical timers are widely used in various applications, such as controlling lights, appliances, irrigation systems, or industrial machinery.

There are different types of electrical timers

1. Mechanical Electrical Timers

These use gears and springs to control the time settings and can be manually adjusted.
When the timer reaches the preset time, it activates or deactivates the electrical circuit.

2. Digital Electrical Timers:

These timers feature a digital display and use electronic circuits to count time accurately.
They often come with more advanced features like multiple settings, countdown options, and precise programming.

3. Programmable Timers:

These timers allow users to set specific times for the circuit to operate on a daily or weekly schedule.
They are often used in smart home devices and industrial systems for automation.



Electrical timers are commonly used in situations where automation is needed, such as in lighting systems
Heating systems, and security devices.
They help conserve energy, increase convenience, and enhance safety by ensuring devices operate only when needed.
