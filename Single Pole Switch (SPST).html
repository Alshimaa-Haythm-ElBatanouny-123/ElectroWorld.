Single Pole Switch (SPST)




A single pole switch is one of the most common types of electrical switches,
designed to control a single electrical circuit from one location.

This type of switch has two terminals

one for the incoming wire (live) and one for the outgoing wire (to the load, such as a light or appliance).

Features

Simple Operation:
The switch either opens or closes the circuit, allowing or stopping the flow of electricity to the connected device.

Control from One Location:
It only allows control of the circuit from one place, meaning you can't control the same device from multiple locations.

Common Use:
It is often used for basic on/off control of lights or small appliances in a room, such as lamps or fans.

How It Works

When the switch is in the "off" position, it disconnects the electrical flow, breaking the circuit.
When turned "on," the switch connects the circuit, allowing the current to flow and power the connected device.

This type of switch is ideal for simple, single-location control of electrical devices,
and it is typically found in most household applications.
