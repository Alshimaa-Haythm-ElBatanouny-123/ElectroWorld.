LED



An LED (Light Emitting Diode) is a semiconductor device that emits light when current flows through it.

How To Work


It works on the principle of electroluminescence, where the semiconductor material releases energy in the form of photons
when excited by an electric current.
LEDs are known for their energy efficiency, long lifespan, and small size compared
To traditional light sources like incandescent bulbs. They are commonly used in various applications, including display screens, indicators, lighting, and even
in modern electronics and communication systems.

LEDs come in different colors, depending on the materials used and are available in various shapes and sizes to suit different needs.
