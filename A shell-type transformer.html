Shell-type transformer



A shell-type transformer (also known as a shell-core transformer) is a type of transformer where the core surrounds the windings.
unlike the core-type transformer where the windings are wrapped around the core.
In a shell-type transformer, the magnetic core is designed in a way that the primary and secondary windings are placed in the central section of the core.
which helps provide better magnetic flux containment.

Here's how it works

Core Design:
The core of a shell-type transformer is made of laminated sheets of magnetic material.
similar to the core-type transformer, but with a different arrangement.
The core structure surrounds the windings from three sides, forming a "shell" around the windings.
This provides better containment of the magnetic flux, improving efficiency.

Magnetic Flux Path

In a shell-type transformer, the magnetic flux travels through the core and surrounds the windings more effectively.
The windings are placed inside the core, with a smaller area of the core exposed to the outside, which reduces energy losses.

Windings

Both the primary and secondary windings are located within the central part of the core, and the magnetic flux flows through them.
This configuration allows for more effective energy transfer between the windings.

Advantages

Shell-type transformers tend to have better performance in terms of reducing losses like eddy
currents and hysteresis because the magnetic path is more concentrated.
This design also offers higher mechanical strength and greater resistance to short circuits compared to core-type transformers.

Applications

Shell-type transformers are commonly used in applications where reduced energy losses and enhanced durability are important.
They are often found in medium to high-power transformers, industrial power supplies, and large electrical systems.

In summary

A shell-type transformer has a core that envelops the windings, which improves the magnetic flux containment and reduces losses.
It is more robust and efficient, making it suitable for high-power applications where efficiency and mechanical strength are critical.
