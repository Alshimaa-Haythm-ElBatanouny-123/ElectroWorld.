Buzzer



A buzzer is an electronic device used to produce sound.
It typically works by converting electrical energy into mechanical energy, creating vibrations that generate audible sounds.
Buzzers are commonly used in various applications, such as alarms, timers, or notification systems.

There are two main types of buzzers

1. Piezoelectric Buzzer:
Uses a piezoelectric element that vibrates when voltage is applied, producing sound.
It is efficient and commonly used in small devices like watches or alarms.

2. Electromechanical Buzzer:
Contains a magnetic coil and diaphragm that vibrates when current flows through the coil.
This type is louder and is often used in industrial or automotive applications.

Buzzers: are easy to use in circuits and can operate on a wide range of voltages.
Making them a versatile component in electronics projects.
