How do work

It consists of a thin wire or strip that melts and breaks the circuit if the current exceeds a safe level.
This prevents damage to the electrical components and reduces the risk of fire or electrical hazards.
Fuses are commonly used in household appliances, automotive systems, and industrial equipment.
Once a fuse blows, it needs to be replaced to restore the circuit.
