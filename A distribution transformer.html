Distribution transformer



A distribution transformer is a type of transformer used in electrical distribution
systems to step down high voltage from transmission lines to a lower voltage suitable for use by consumers.
It plays a crucial role in the final stage of electricity delivery,
making electrical energy usable for homes, businesses, and industrial facilities.

Working Principle

A distribution transformer operates on the principle of electromagnetic induction , similar to other transformers.
It consists of two coils: the primary winding (connected to the high-voltage supply) and the secondary winding (connected to the low-voltage load).
The transformer reduces the voltage from the high level used in transmission lines to a safer, lower level for residential or industrial use.

Key Features

1. Voltage Step-down:
The main function of a distribution transformer is to reduce the high voltage
(typically 11kV to 33kV) to a lower voltage (e.g., 230V or 400V in most households).

2. Efficiency:
Distribution transformers are designed for high efficiency and low losses, ensuring minimal energy wastage during the voltage conversion.

3. Insulation:
The transformer uses high-quality insulation to prevent electrical faults and to ensure safety.

4. Cooling:
These transformers are usually oil-immersed or air-cooled to regulate their temperature during operation.

Types of Distribution Transformers

1. Oil-immersed transformers:
These are commonly used and are filled with oil to provide cooling and insulation.
The oil absorbs the heat produced during operation.

2. Dry-type transformers:
These transformers do not use oil and are cooled by air.
They are used in areas where oil-filled transformers are not suitable due to safety or environmental concerns.

Applications

Residential areas:
They convert the voltage from the distribution grid to a safe level for homes and small businesses.

Industrial facilities:
They supply power at appropriate voltages for manufacturing processes and heavy machinery.

Commercial establishments:
They ensure safe and reliable power supply to office buildings, shopping malls, and other commercial units.

In summary

A distribution transformer is a key component in electrical power systems,
ensuring that high-voltage electricity is converted to a usable level for everyday use.
